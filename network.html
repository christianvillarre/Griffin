Network & Affiliates

Griffin Integrity Solutions works with a select network of technology providers, equipment manufacturers, automation specialists, and technical service organizations to support advanced inspection, continuous monitoring, and asset integrity initiatives. These relationships help clients evaluate, source, and implement specialized NDT products, services, and subject matter expertise across diverse market sectors.

Danatronics Corp.

U.S. manufacturer of advanced NDT technologies specializing in portable ultrasonic testing (UT) equipment and instrumentation, including portable touch screen and keypad operated flaw detectors, corrosion and precision UT thickness gages, transducers, and accessories.

Guided Ultrasonics Ltd.

Large area guided wave monitoring technologies designed to support long-term asset condition visibility, pipeline integrity program management, and automated remote monitoring applications.

Sound NDT Solutions LLC

Advanced ultrasonic testing solutions, specializing in phased array and automated ultrasonic weld inspection techniques and systems. Providing advanced NDT field and laboratory testing services across diverse market sectors and industrial environments.

Top Notch EDM Services LLC

Precision machined and welded specialty products and services supporting advanced NDT service providers and testing labs, including reference standards and calibration blocks, custom welded flaw samples, corrosion specimens, and automated 5-Axis machining services.
